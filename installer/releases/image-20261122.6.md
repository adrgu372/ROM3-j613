# ROM3-j613 installer payload 20261122.6

Native installer payload assembled from the verified
`image-20261122.5` filesystem image and the ROM3 Recovery template.

The payload is restricted to `apple,j613` by the signed ROM3 catalog. It
contains the native installer ZIP in GitHub-sized parts, the `os_list`
installer metadata, `delivery.json`, a payload manifest, signed checksums once
the catalog publication step completes, and a reassembly script.

This is a testing payload. It is not evidence of a clean install, first boot,
cold boot or Recovery qualification on J613.
