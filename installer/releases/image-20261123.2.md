# ROM3-j613 installer payload 20261123.2

Native installer payload assembled from the verified `image-20261123.1`
filesystem image and the ROM3 `0.1.3` Recovery template.

The payload uses `stable-20261122.9` and is restricted to `apple,j613` by the
signed ROM3 catalog. It includes automatic ROM3 keyring restoration and the
external signed-override guard.

The native ZIP is published in GitHub-sized parts together with `os_list`
metadata, `delivery.json`, payload provenance, signed checksums and a
reassembly helper.

This remains a testing payload pending a complete clean-install, every-boot
and Recovery qualification cycle on J613.
